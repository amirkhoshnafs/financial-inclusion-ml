# Concept glossary

## Financial inclusion

Financial inclusion means people can access and use useful financial services such as accounts, payments, savings, credit, and tools for managing financial risk.

## Account ownership

Account ownership means a person has an account at a financial institution or through a mobile money provider.

## Financial exclusion

Financial exclusion means a person lacks access to formal or useful financial services.

## Digital payments

Digital payments measure active use of financial services through digital channels.

## Mobile money

Mobile money allows people to store, send, and receive money using a mobile phone.

## Formal saving

Formal saving means saving through a formal financial institution or regulated provider.

## Formal borrowing

Formal borrowing means borrowing from a formal financial institution or regulated lender.

## Financial resilience

Financial resilience means the ability to handle financial shocks such as emergency expenses or income loss.

## Demographic gaps

The main gaps studied in this project are gender, income, education, age, and geography.

## Policy intervention

A policy intervention is an action that could plausibly reduce financial exclusion, such as mobile access, digital literacy, lower fees, ID access, or trust-building.
